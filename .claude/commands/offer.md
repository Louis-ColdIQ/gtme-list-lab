# /offer

Help the student turn what they sell into a Mafia Offer: an offer so good the buyer feels stupid saying no. Follow the `offer-builder` skill if it is available. If not, follow the steps below.

## Rules

1. Read `clients/<client>/client.md` first. If it does not exist, run `new-client` first.
2. One question at a time. Plain words. No em dashes or en dashes.
3. Never invent proof, numbers, or guarantees.
4. Do not put a price in the outreach offer. Keep the first ask small.

## Steps

1. Confirm the client folder.
2. Ask, one at a time, for anything `client.md` does not already make clear: what they sell, who the buyer is, the dream outcome, a specific result and timeframe, what they can give away or guarantee, how little the buyer has to do, and any real proof.
3. Replace the `## Offer` section in `client.md` with: the problem, dream outcome, specific result and timeframe, risk reversal, the small first step, one sentence for cold outreach, and 3 one-line offer variants.
4. Show the draft, let them pick the main variant, then write the file.
5. Offer to save it to GitHub, and ask before any commit or push.
