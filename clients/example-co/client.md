# Example Co (made up example)

This client is fictional. It shows what a good `client.md` looks like. Do not use it for real outreach.

## Basics
- Website: example.com (placeholder)
- What they sell: ShiftPal, a simple staff scheduling app for independent coffee shops.
- Who I talk to there: Sam Example, founder

## ICP (who they sell to)
- Industry: independent coffee shops and small cafe groups
- Company size: 5 to 50 staff, 1 to 5 locations
- Countries: United Kingdom, Ireland
- Signs a company is a great fit: hiring baristas right now, opening a new location, posts rotas on social media, open 7 days a week
- Job titles to reach: Owner, Founder, Operations Manager, General Manager

## Offer
- The main problem they solve: rotas are built in spreadsheets and group chats, so shifts get missed and owners lose hours every week.
- The result a customer gets: rota done in 15 minutes a week and fewer no shows.
- The offer for cold outreach: a free 30 day trial, set up for them in one call.

## Proof
- Customers: "Bean There" (3 sites, Leeds), "Morning Ritual" (1 site, Dublin). Both made up.
- Numbers: owners save about 3 hours a week on rotas (made up).
- Story: Bean There went from a WhatsApp rota to ShiftPal in a day and stopped double booking staff.

## Tone
- Friendly, plain, a bit warm. Sounds like a person, not a brand.
- Words to use: rota, shifts, staff, simple
- Words to avoid: synergy, leverage, revolutionary, game changer

## Exclusions (who to skip)
- Big chains (more than 20 locations)
- Restaurants and bars (different scheduling needs)
- Existing customers: Bean There, Morning Ritual

## Notes
- The founder prefers short emails with one question at the end.
